# User personas

Everyone who uses ORBIT '26 is an attendee; what differs is how long they are
there, how much they plan, and whether they are on stage too. The seed creates
one or two accounts for each, so a feature can be tried as the person it is for
(pick them from the attendee switcher). Their ids, days and the test fixtures
they hold are in [`seed.md`](../engineering-context/seed.md).

## The all-week VIP

Here for all four days on a VIP ticket, with the fullest agenda — and so the
most clashes, full rooms and trips between the two venues.

- **Needs:** to see the whole week at a glance, swap clashing talks quickly,
  and know when to leave for the other venue.
- **Seeded as:** Sofia Almeida, Head of Developer Experience (the biggest
  plan); Jonas Claesson, AI Software Engineer (all four days, the cross-town
  timings).

## The short-stay attendee

A standard ticket for two days, with a light plan: a few talks chosen in
advance, often the keynote.

- **Needs:** to find the few sessions worth the trip, keep a seat in them, and
  not miss the keynote.
- **Seeded as:** Kenji Nakamura, Staff ML Engineer (always at the keynote);
  Marcus Whitfield, VP of Engineering (the smallest plan).

## The speaking attendee

On a speaker ticket: presenting sessions of their own, and attending others
around them. Their attendee account is linked to their speaker profile.

- **Needs:** their own talks and the talks they want to see in one schedule,
  without booking something that clashes with their own slot.
- **Seeded as:** Andy Vays, Technical Principal (three sessions); Abraham
  Marín Pérez, AI Software Engineer (two sessions).
