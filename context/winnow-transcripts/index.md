# Winnow transcripts

One record per conversation Winnow has processed — a meeting, a voice note, a
brainstorm, product or technical. Some of what was said became tickets, some
went into the context, and some was deliberately set aside. The record keeps
all three, so "why did we decide not to do that?" has an answer a month later.

A record says:

- **The conversation** — its title, date, participants, and where the source
  lives (a link, or the transcript itself).
- **What came out of it** — the tickets raised, with links, and the context
  documents updated.
- **What was set aside, and why.**
- **The questions Winnow asked** and how they were answered.

Name a record `YYYY-MM-DD-<short-title>.md`, so the folder reads in date order.

This folder sits at the top of `context/`, not under product, because Winnow
can process an architecture discussion just as well as a product one.
