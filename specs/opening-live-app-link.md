# Point attendees at the live app from the opening talk

Slide 11 of the opening presentation (`/opening-presentation?beat=11`, the
"Today" plan) gains a panel under the plan with two things attendees need
before the labs:

- **Try the live app** — a link to `https://orbit26.up.railway.app/`, opening in
  a new tab. The click stops propagation, so it does not advance the deck.
- **Where to start** — Home and My Agenda are left raw on purpose (CLAUDE.md,
  *Deliberately unbuilt*), so teams may want to build on them first.

To make room, the five plan cards are 40px shorter (they were mostly empty at
the bottom) and the "one shared context" rail and its connectors move up with
them, so the panel clears the deck's progress bar.

## Out of scope

No other slide, deck or behaviour changes. No new link is invented: the
Railway URL is the one the README already publishes.
